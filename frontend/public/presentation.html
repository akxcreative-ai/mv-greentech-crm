Slide 05 • Sales Leadership

Sales Head Workspace (/sales-head/dashboard)

Supervising pipeline velocity, allocating leads across 4 representatives, and preventing margin erosion.

1. Dynamic Lead Balancing
When new inquiries arrive via web, phone, or Indiamart, the Sales Head selects an available sales representative and assigns the account with 1 click.
2. Quotation Margin Approvals
Any quotation with discounts or custom credit terms is routed to the Sales Head for margin verification before the client receives the PDF.
3. Overdue Follow-Up Radar
If any representative fails to contact a client on their scheduled date, the deal is flagged in bright red on the Sales Head desk for audit.
4. Quota Allocation & Velocity
Sets and reviews monthly revenue quotas for the 4 representatives, ensuring team targets stay on track.
